Around The Block LLC

Website security reviews

A point-in-time look at your public website, with human-reviewed findings and suggested fixes.

We use AI-assisted tools to look at what a normal visitor can already see. A person reviews every finding before it is shared. This is a snapshot of the site on the day it is tested. It is not monitoring, not a certification, and not a guarantee that the site is secure.

Free for DigiByte builders.

If you publicly build or maintain a DigiByte project, your review is free. Free reviews get the same care and checks as any other review. Each one covers the site you name, within the scope we agree on together.

Other projects: tell us about your site and we will follow up.

How it works

  1. Prepare a request with the form below. It opens your email app with a draft. It does not send the message by itself, and it does not start a review.
  2. We confirm you control the site, with a DNS record or a small file we give you.
  3. We agree on scope in writing before any work. We only look at what a normal visitor can see unless you sign a separate authorization for deeper testing.
  4. You get your findings privately, by a channel agreed for that project.

Preparing this request is not permission to test. Nothing starts until we confirm with you. We never ask for passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or payment details. Do not send them.

How findings are shared

Findings are shared privately, by a channel agreed for that project. We do not publish them without your written consent. Secrets are redacted in the report. We never ask for your password, and you should not send one.

The badge

If you want one after a review, you can show a dated badge on your site:

Reviewed by AroundTheBlock.Us on the review date

  • It says reviewed, never secured. It is not a certification.
  • It links to a verification page with the review date, expiry date, and a plain disclaimer.
  • It expires 90 days after the review. A re-review renews it.
  • We can revoke it if it is misused, and it comes down when it expires.

What is not included

  • Continuous monitoring, firewalls, malware cleanup, or incident response.
  • Any promise that your site is secure or free of problems.
  • Fixing the issues. You get findings and guidance. The fixes are yours unless we agree otherwise.
  • Anything that changes your data, denial of service, or social engineering.
  • Your host, CDN, or cloud provider, unless you get their permission first.

The fine print

  1. Default Services. Unless the parties agree otherwise in writing, the Services consist of testing of Client's public-facing websites and related public surfaces using current AI-assisted toolkits, operated and supervised by a person. The specific tools and methods may expand or change over time. By default, testing is passive and non-intrusive: it is limited to what a normal browser user or public DNS lookup could observe, plus materials Client chooses to provide.
  2. Active or Targeted Testing. ATB will perform deeper, targeted, or active testing (for example authenticated testing, port scanning, fuzzing, or exploit-style checks) only if Client requests it in writing and the parties sign a written authorization and rules of engagement that name the assets, methods, time window, rate limits, and contacts. ATB will stay within those rules and will stop on Client's request.
  3. Point-in-Time Review. Each review reflects only the systems, configurations, and content observed on the date or dates tested. The Services are not continuous monitoring, incident response, or ongoing protection. Changes made after the test date are not covered.
  4. No Warranty. The Services, reports, and any badge are provided "as is." ATB makes no warranty or guarantee, express or implied, that any site is secure, free of defects, or free of vulnerabilities, and disclaims all implied warranties, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose. Tools and reviewers can miss issues. Findings are advisory only. Client alone is responsible for deciding whether and how to fix any issue and for all remediation.
  5. Limitation of Liability. To the fullest extent permitted by law, ATB's liability for an engagement is limited as stated in the written agreement for that engagement. In no event is ATB liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue, data, or goodwill, even if advised of their possibility. This section does not limit liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.
  6. Client Authority and Verification. Before any testing, Client must confirm in writing that it owns, or is authorized by the owner to permit testing of, every asset in scope. ATB will verify control of each domain by a DNS TXT record or a file placed at a /.well-known path that ATB specifies, and will not test until verification succeeds. Client will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless ATB from claims, losses, and costs arising from any misstatement of Client's ownership or authority.
  7. Third-Party Infrastructure. Client is responsible for obtaining any permission required from its hosting provider, CDN, cloud platform, or other third-party service before testing. Third-party systems are out of scope unless Client provides that provider's written permission or the provider's published terms clearly allow the testing.
  8. Confidentiality and Data Handling. ATB will treat findings, reports, and data collected during an engagement as Client confidential information, use them only to perform the Services, and not publish or share them without Client's written consent, except as required by law. ATB will collect the minimum data needed, redact secrets in reports, protect stored data with reasonable security measures, keep raw tool output for up to 90 days after delivery and final reports for up to 24 months (or less on Client's written request), and then delete them.
  9. AI-Assisted Tools. ATB uses automated and AI-assisted tools to run tests and draft reports. A person reviews findings and reports before delivery, but AI-assisted output can contain errors, and that review does not change Sections 3 through 5.
  10. Badge. If Client opts in, ATB may issue a badge that states the site was "reviewed," never "secured," for example: "Reviewed by AroundTheBlock.Us on YYYY-MM-DD (badge expires YYYY-MM-DD)." The badge must link to ATB's verification page, which shows the review date, expiry, and a disclaimer that a review is not a guarantee of security. The badge expires 90 days after the review date unless renewed by a new review. ATB may revoke the badge at any time, including if Client misrepresents it, and Client will remove it on expiry or revocation and will not describe it as a certification or guarantee.
