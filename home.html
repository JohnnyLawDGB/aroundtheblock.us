Products

DigiByte Wallet

Android · Non-custodial · Open source

A non-custodial mobile wallet for Android. Your keys are generated and stored on your phone, and sync runs on BIP157/158 compact block filters, so your addresses never leave your device. Coming to Google Play. An iOS version is in development.

DigiScope

Explorer · Analytics · Education

A block explorer, on-chain analytics platform and education site for the DigiByte network. It includes exchange and network analytics. Trace addresses and transactions, browse DigiAssets, and learn how the chain works. Free to read, with no account required.

Infrastructure

DigiByte network infrastructure

We have been part of the DigiByte community for more than eight years. Alongside our products, we help keep the network running.

  • We run a seeder node for the DigiByte network.
  • We updated Electrum servers for DigiByte.
  • We built a Rosetta server implementation, in partnership with AI.
  • We signed the 8.22 release.
  • We tested the 9.26 release and contributed bug reports and fixes that shipped in the final version.
  • We take part in the current DigiByte developer chats on Gitter.
  • We maintain and secure the price oracles that DigiDollar minting depends on.
  • We can trace stolen funds on-chain.
