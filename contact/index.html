WHAT WE BUILD

DigiByte Wallet is a non-custodial mobile wallet for DigiByte. Sync runs on BIP157/158 compact block filters, so the wallet's addresses never leave your device, and your keys are generated and stored on your phone. Open source, with reproducible builds. Available for Android, with iOS in development.

github.com/JohnnyLawDGB/digibytewallet-android

DigiScope is our block explorer, on-chain analytics and educational platform for the DigiByte network.

digiscope.me
